Nu AMFT

Privacy Policy · Effective date: August 2, 2026

Overview

Nu AMFT helps you record garden beds, planting steps, spacing estimates, and related notes on your device. This policy describes how the app handles information. We designed Nu AMFT so that your garden data stays under your control on your phone or tablet.

All user data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or share any personal information with us or with third parties.

Information stored on your device

The app may store the following types of data only on your device, for your own use:

  • Bed and crop labels, measurements, categories, and notes you enter
  • Planting process entries (dates, stages, notes, and optional photos you choose to attach)
  • Spacing and yield calculation results you save
  • Photos you capture or select for beds or process entries, saved in the app’s local storage area
  • Custom category names and preferences you set in Settings

You may export a backup file (JSON) when you use the export feature; sharing that file is entirely your choice and occurs outside the app through system share sheets you control.

How your data is used

Nu AMFT processes the information you enter only on your device to show your bed lists, garden summaries, planting timelines, spacing math, and history views. Calculations and charts are generated from your saved records locally; nothing is sent to the developer for processing or storage.

Optional export creates a file on your device that you may save or share through iOS. Import reads a file you select and merges or replaces local records according to your choice in the app. Those actions stay under your control and on your device unless you deliberately share a file elsewhere.

Permissions

If you choose to take photos or pick images from your photo library, the app requests camera or photo library access only for that purpose. Images you attach are stored locally with your records unless you delete them or remove the app.

Data retention and deletion

Data remains on your device until you edit or delete it in the app, use “Delete all local data” in Settings, or uninstall Nu AMFT. Uninstalling the app removes locally stored app data from your device subject to iOS behavior and any device backups you maintain.

Children’s privacy

Nu AMFT is a general-purpose garden planning and record-keeping tool. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because data is not transmitted to us, there is no collection on our side to opt out of.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page will change when we do. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
